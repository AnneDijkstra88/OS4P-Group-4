
## Step 1: Insert the threaded rods
Start with the bottom plate of the gearbox. Insert the four threaded rods into the holes at each corner of the bottom plate, as shown in Figure 1.
Figure 1: Bottom plate with the four threaded rods installed.
![step1](foto1.jpeg)
## Step 2: Place the gears
Place the gears onto their corresponding shafts on the bottom plate, as shown in Figure 2. Make sure that the teeth of neighbouring gears properly engage with each other.
Place the pillars into the rods as shown in the picture. 
Figure 2: Placement of the gears on the bottom plate.
![step2](foto2.jpeg)
## Step 3: Install the drive gear
Place the drive gear and its shaft in the corresponding position, as shown in Figure 3. Make sure that the drive gear engages correctly with the neighbouring gear.
Check the complete gear mechanism by manually rotating the drive shaft.
Figure 3: Gear mechanism with the drive gear installed.
![step3](foto3.jpeg)
## Step 4: Attach the top plate
Place the top plate over the gearbox. Guide the four threaded rods through the corresponding corner holes and make sure that the central shaft passes through the opening in the top plate as can be seen in figure 4 and 5.  
Figure 4: Top plate positioned over the assembled gear mechanism.
![step4](foto4.jpeg)
![step5](foto5.jpeg)
## Step 5: Secure the gearbox
Place a nut on each of the four threaded rods and tighten them to secure the top and bottom plates together.
