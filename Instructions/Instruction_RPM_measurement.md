After you have completed the gearbox and made the wheel spin, it is time to measure how fast the wheel is spinning.

## Step 1: Make sure you have arduino installed on your laptop.

## Step 2: Copy the Tachometer script from the software folder to your arduino.

## Step 3: Connect the laptop to the arduino and tape the sensor on the black housing of the gearbox.

## Step 4: Make sure that the black sensor is right under the edge of the spinning wheel.

## Step 5: Upload the script and click on 'Serial monitor' to watch the RPM.
