Instructions for assembling the gearbox.

Once you have completed the 3D printing, it is time to assemble the gearbox. Everything you need other than the components you just 3D printed is added in the list
of materials in this folder. 

## Step 1: 
Take the top plate and put the motor through the hole shown in the picture. It is important that the moter is fit tightly against the top plate. Therefore,
we use two short bolts of 2.5 mm and use a drill to screw them through the top plate and motor via the small holes on the side.
![Description](foto1.png)
![Description](foto2.png)


## Step 2: 
Take the smallest gear and place it on top of the motor.
![Description](foto3.png)

## Step 3: 
Place the middle long bolt through the middle hole and the long bolt through the upper hole. It is important that these bolts are held in place from 
the bottom. For this, we used two pieces of tape.
![Description](foto4.png)
![Description](foto5.png)
## Step 4: 
Place the gears alternating on the middle long bolt and the long bolt. It is important that the part of the gear with the largest diameter is on the 
bottom every time. To fill up the spaces, we used flat metal washers. This eventually reduces friction and instability. You will need to experiment yourself with the amount of rings
you need for optimal working.
![Description](foto6.png)
![Description](foto7.png)
## Step 5: 
Place the gear with the long stick on top on the long screw.
![Description](foto8.png)
![Description](foto9.png)

## Step 6: 
Prepare the top plate by placing a ball bearing inside the largest hole. If needed, you can glue the ball bearing inside the hole.
![Description](foto10.png)

## Step 7: 
Place 4 middle long bolts through the holes in the corner and place the pillars on top.
![Description](foto1.png)

## Step 8: 
Place the top plate on top and use the hex nuts on top of the screws in the corners.
![Description](foto11.png)
